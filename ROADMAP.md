Where Tāne Sovereign OS Harness is now, and what comes next. We build in three phases: first make every screen look exactly as designed, then make everything flow and feel right, then grow the wider harness.

## Phase 2: the experience

How everything flows and feels, once it all looks right.

- Moving around by voice on every screen ("take me to…", "stop")
- Tāne guiding you aloud on each screen
- Train Me: teaching Tāne your own voice and the way you speak
- Any idea, designed with Tāne: growing any idea, not just a thesis, in the digital garden
- Draw: making and editing images on your own devices
- Better understanding of te reo Māori

## Phase 3: the wider harness

- Wānanga mode: learning and teaching together
- Home Nodes for marae (community meeting grounds), iwi (tribes) and communities, so people without their own computer can still keep their data sovereign
- Web search with sources you can check
- Hawaiki Hōu: the shared world, where everyone can hold their own piece of land, mountain and river
- In the long term, Tāne's own devices

## How we'll share it

1. **Write the journal:** the meaning behind each part, in Thomas's words.
2. **Split the code:** the free engine, the protected cultural pack, and the paid modules.
3. **Go public** at the end of phase 1.
4. **Build community:** discussions, starter tasks, and replying to everyone.
