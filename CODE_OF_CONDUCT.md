# Code of conduct

This is a kaupapa Māori project. Everyone who takes part agrees to:

- treat people with manaakitanga: be kind, patient and respectful;
- respect tikanga, te reo Māori and mātauranga, and the people who hold them;
- welcome newcomers, and help without talking down;
- disagree about the work, never attack the person;
- never share anyone's personal, whānau or cultural information without their consent.

Harassment, racism, or disrespect of Māori culture are not accepted. The maintainers may remove comments, code or people who don't follow this. Report concerns privately to the maintainers.
