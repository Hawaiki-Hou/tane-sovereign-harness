# Whitepaper: Owning Your Own AI

*The short version of why Tāne Sovereign OS Harness exists. The full whitepaper and the research behind it are linked at the end.*

## The problem

AI is becoming the way we search, learn, write, create and remember. Right now almost all of it runs in the clouds of a few very large companies. To use it, you send them your words, your voice, your photos and your knowledge. They keep it, learn from it, and profit from it.

For Indigenous peoples this is an old story in a new form: our knowledge taken, stored somewhere else, and used without us. But it is everyone's problem. Anyone who writes, makes music, runs a small business or simply talks to their phone is feeding systems they do not own.

## Our answer

**Tāne Sovereign OS Harness** is a free, open-source AI that you own. It runs on your phone and your own home computer first, and only reaches the cloud when you say so.

- **Local first.** The thinking, listening, speaking and remembering all happen on your own devices, even with the internet switched off.
- **You hold the key.** Nothing leaves without your permission, and anything that does leave is encrypted before it goes.
- **Sacred stays home.** Private and sacred conversations are recognised and never sent to the cloud, by design rather than by promise.
- **Open to check.** The code is public so anyone can confirm we do what we say.

## Data sovereignty

Data sovereignty means self-determination over your own information: the right to decide for yourself. Applied to data, it means the people a piece of knowledge comes from decide where it lives, who sees it, and how it is used.

Tāne was designed through a Māori lens, but the principle is universal. Every person should have authority over their own data and content, and should be the one who benefits from it.

## Five pillars of wellbeing

The interface is built around a Māori model of balance and health. Tāne keeps an eye on all five, not just productivity:

1. **Spirit:** purpose and meaning
2. **Mind:** thinking and learning
3. **Body:** physical health
4. **Community:** family, friends and relationships
5. **Environment:** the world around you

## Hawaiki Hōu: where it's heading

Hawaiki Hōu (New Hawaiki, our ancestral homeland reimagined) is a shared digital world: a place to meet, learn and create together, where everyone can hold their own piece of land, mountain and river. Tāne is the guide who takes you there. The harness is the foundation; Hawaiki Hōu is the destination.

## Money flows down, not up

Most platforms let money flow up from the people to the top 1%. Ours is built the other way:

- **Free for everyone:** the Harness, the Tāne Dashboard, Train Me, Hawaiki Hōu and My Apps.
- **Subscriptions** pay for the specialist tools: Te Māra Matihiko (for researchers), the Data Guardian features, IP protection and Tāne Media.
- **Creators keep 97%** of what they earn through Tāne Media. The 3% fee keeps the lights on.
- **Te Pā Tūwatawata** (named for a pā tūwatawata, a fortified place of strength and security that defends and provides for those within) is an iwi-designed, Māori-owned data storage network, developed by Te Kāhui Raraunga. Your data stays on your own devices first. Te Pā Tūwatawata is an extra option, for people who run out of space on their own phone and computer, or who want a backup in case a device breaks or is replaced: a safe, encrypted place whose principles align with Māori data sovereignty. We plan to offer it once an agreement is in place, with a small amount free.

People benefit from their own data and content, instead of big tech profiting from it.

## What we keep private, and why

The engine is open. Some cultural material is not: certain designs, carvings and knowledge belong to their people and are shared only with their blessing. These live in a protected cultural pack, separate from the open code. Being open about the code and careful with the culture are both part of the same promise.
