# Security

Tāne holds people's research, whānau information and mātauranga. We take security reports seriously.

## Report a problem privately
**Please don't open a public issue.** Use GitHub's private reporting instead: go to the **Security** tab of this repository and choose **"Report a vulnerability"**.

Include:
- what you found and where (file, screen or endpoint);
- how to reproduce it;
- what someone could do with it;
- your name, if you'd like credit.

## What happens next
- We reply within **7 days**.
- We agree a fix and a date with you before anything is made public.
- We credit you when the fix is released, unless you'd rather stay anonymous.

## Especially important
- anything that lets data leave the device or the Home Node without the person's consent;
- anything tapu, or any tamariki data, being exposed;
- forged or replayed invites, acceptances, endings or notes;
- the sovereignty guard or private mode being bypassed.

## Please don't
- test against other people's devices or data;
- run denial-of-service tests against shared services;
- share the problem publicly before it's fixed.
