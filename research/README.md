# Research and Publications

The thinking behind Tāne, by Dr Thomas Clark.

## Papers

- **Hawaiki Hōu: Artificial Intelligence or Augmented Intelligence?** (2026)
  Big tech platforms act like digital countries. This paper argues that digital sovereignty begins with digital whenua (land): Indigenous-controlled servers, storage, platforms and rules. It introduces Hawaiki Hōu as a Māori-led platform where AI works as augmented intelligence, a helper governed by the community.
  [Read the paper (PDF)](hawaiki-hou-augmented-intelligence.pdf)
