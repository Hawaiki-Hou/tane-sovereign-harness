# Our values

**Our North Star.** Knowledge and data belong to the people they come from. This harness exists so families, communities and anyone else can use AI without handing their lives to big tech.

- **Self-determination:** each person decides what is shared, with whom, and when.
- **Your devices first:** everything works on your own phone and computer before any cloud is touched.
- **Indigenous data sovereignty:** Indigenous data is governed by Indigenous people.
- **A Māori lens, open to all:** the design comes from a Māori world view, and everyone is welcome to use it with respect.
- **A living system:** the harness follows the 5-Pou (five pillars) wellbeing framework. It responds to how we behave, alone and together, to help us build healthier habits online.
- **Cultural treasures are protected:** cultural designs, carvings, tattoo art and traditional knowledge are shared only with permission, under their own licence.

**How we treat each other:** with care, respect for culture, and kindness in every review and conversation.
